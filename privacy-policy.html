Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

Tap On It ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tap On It mobile application. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, email address, and phone number when you register.
  • Location: precise GPS location while an order is active — used to connect you with nearby vendors and share your position with your assigned driver.
  • Order information: items ordered, delivery address, and order totals. Payments are processed by third-party providers; we do not store raw card numbers.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, crash reports, and anonymised usage analytics.
  • Push notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send you order status alerts. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To share your live delivery location with your assigned driver during active deliveries.
  • To send order status push notifications.
  • To improve app features using anonymised analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Vendors & drivers: your name, delivery address, and order details to fulfil your order.
  • Firebase (Google): authentication, push notifications, and crash reporting.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers: under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Law enforcement: where required by applicable law.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. Completed order records are retained for up to 3 years for accounting and dispute resolution. If you delete your account, personal identifiers are removed within 30 days; anonymised order statistics may be retained.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To delete your account:

  • Open the App → Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete My Account".
  • Your account and personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

You may also email nteintech@gmail.com for any data requests.

6. Security

All data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. We follow OWASP security best practices. No online transmission method is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Tap On It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us immediately if you believe a child has provided personal data and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via push notification or in-app message. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
